Privacy Policy for Zip Game

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Last Updated: October 18, 2026

At a Glance: Zip Game is built on strict data minimalism. We do not sell your personal data, we do not perform cross-app behavioral advertising, and all online gameplay features use pseudonymous random identifiers. You can reset or delete all your data at any time directly in the app.

1. Summary of Principles

  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.
  • Minimalist Data Collection: We do not collect your real name, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS location.
  • Zero Behavioral Ad Tracking: The App contains no third-party ad networks tracking you across apps or websites.
  • Full User Control: You can reset and delete all local progress and associated cloud records at any time.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The vast majority of gameplay data resides exclusively on your local device via secure on-device storage:

  • Gameplay Progress: Completed level indices, star ratings, move counts, and Par performance metrics.
  • Player Preferences: Dark mode, reduced motion, tactile haptics, sound effects, procedural soundscapes, and color symbol stencils.
  • Session State: In-progress level undo/redo differential state records.
  • Archival Passport & Badges: Unlocked curatorial achievements, trophies, and cryptographic Merkle root hash tokens representing puzzle milestones.

B. Information Processed via Online Cloud Features (Optional)

When you choose to use online features (such as Global Leaderboards, Async 1v1 Curator Duels, Cloud Save Sync, or the Community Workshop):

  • Anonymous Player Identifier (UUID): A randomly generated pseudonymous client UUID created on your device during initial setup. It is never linked to your personal Google or Apple accounts.
  • Curator Alias (Nickname): An optional display name you choose for leaderboards and community puzzles (defaulting to "Anonymous Curator").
  • Level Telemetry & Scores: Number of moves, elapsed time, Par status, and level timestamps for leaderboard rankings.
  • Community Workshop Creations: Custom puzzle grid strings, author attribution names, and difficulty ratings you deliberately publish to the community gallery.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: Providing online leaderboard competition, asynchronous multiplayer duels, and cloud backup as requested.
  • Legitimate Interests: Ensuring fair play (anti-cheese verification), detecting server abuse, maintaining data integrity, and fixing software crashes.
  • Consent: Where required by applicable privacy laws, you consent to submitting public aliases or workshop puzzle designs.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our cloud infrastructure reliably, we use:

  • Supabase Inc.: Provides secure PostgreSQL database and REST API infrastructure for cloud saves, global leaderboards, and curator duels. Data transmission is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Supabase adheres to strict enterprise data protection standards (SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001).

No other third-party analytics trackers, behavioral ad SDKs, or social media plugins are included in the App.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Safeguards)

Zip Game is rated for All Ages (3+ / Everyone). It is a family-friendly logic puzzle game.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • Online features use purely pseudonymous identifiers and do not require email addresses, birth dates, or real names.
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us at privacy@zipgame.app and we will promptly delete such records.

6. Data Retention and Account/Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) & GDPR)

You maintain complete ownership of your data:

  • In-App Immediate Deletion: Open Settings in Zip Game → scroll to Data & Privacy → tap "Delete Account & Reset Data". This immediately erases all local completed level archives, star ratings, custom levels, settings, and associated cloud leaderboard submissions linked to your UUID.
  • Deletion via Email: You may also email us at privacy@zipgame.app with your Curatorial Passport token or Player ID, and our team will verify and purge all associated server records within 30 days.

7. Security of Your Information

All client-server communications use HTTPS with modern TLS encryption. Your level completion ledgers are cryptographically hashed (Merkle tree hashes), safeguarding against tampering without exposing personal information.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA

You have the right to access, rectify, port, or erase your data at any time without discrimination.
