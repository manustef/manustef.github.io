2. Datele pe Care le Prelucrăm

În funcție de modul în care utilizați jocul Zip, pot fi prelucrate următoarele categorii de date:

  • Date Locale de Joc (stocate pe dispozitivul dumneavoastră): Nivelele completate, stelele obținute, scorurile par, setările de temă (Light/Dark mode) și de sunet/vibrație. Aceste date nu părăsesc dispozitivul decât dacă alegeți sincronizarea opțională.

3. Rețele Publicitare și Consimțământ (Google AdMob & Apple ATT)

Jocul poate afișa reclame furnizate prin rețeaua oficială Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited):

  • Consimțământul pe iOS (Apple App Tracking Transparency - ATT): Pe dispozitivele Apple, conform Ghidului Apple 5.1.2, vă vom solicita permisiunea înainte de a afișa reclame personalizate. Dacă refuzați, veți primi reclame contextuale fără urmărire între aplicații.
  • Consimțământul pe Android / UE (GDPR): Utilizatorii din Spațiul Economic European pot alege afișarea de reclame personalizate sau non-personalizate.
  • Eliminarea Reclamelor („Remove Ads”): Puteți elimina permanent orice reclamă din joc prin achiziția opțiunii din ecranul de setări al jocului. În acel moment, toate componentele publicitare sunt oprite definitiv.

Această acțiune șterge instantaneu și ireversibil toate datele locale, istoricul și înregistrările din clasament asociate identificatorului dumneavoastră.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 • Data Controller: Novia Prime SRL

At a Glance

Zip strictly respects your privacy. We do not collect or store your real name, physical address, phone number, or payment details. Game progress is saved locally on your device, and optional online features (such as leaderboards) operate using pseudonymous, randomly-generated identifiers.

1. Identity of the Data Controller

The application and associated services are developed and operated by:

Company: Novia Prime SRL
Headquarters: Bucharest, Romania
Official Privacy & Support Email: zipgameapp@gmail.com

2. Data We Process

Depending on your interaction with Zip, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • Local Gameplay Data (stored strictly on your device): Solved stages, star ratings, par performance records, light/dark theme preference, and audio/haptic toggles. This data never leaves your device unless you opt into cloud synchronization.
  • Pseudonymous Player Identifier (UUID): A cryptographically randomized identifier generated upon install to facilitate high-score submissions on public leaderboards without collecting your personal identity.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Telemetry: Device hardware model, operating system version (iOS/Android), and anonymized crash logs utilized to rectify performance bottlenecks.

3. Advertising Networks & Consent (Google AdMob & Apple ATT)

The game may serve advertisements provided through the official Google AdMob network (Google Ireland Limited):

  • Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On Apple iOS devices, pursuant to Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.2, you will be presented with a native consent dialog before personalized advertising is delivered. Denying permission serves non-personalized, contextual ads.
  • European Union (GDPR) Consent: Users in the EEA can choose between personalized and contextual ad delivery at any time.
  • Remove Ads In-App Purchase: You can permanently suppress all ads at any time by acquiring the "Remove Ads" item within the in-game settings. Upon acquisition, all ad daemons are permanently terminated.

4. Account & Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) & GDPR Art. 17)

You retain full sovereignty over your data at all times:

  • Direct In-App Deletion: Navigate to Settings in Zip → locate Data & Privacy → tap "Delete Account & Reset Data". This immediately and irreversibly wipes all local records, star ratings, and disassociates your UUID from online leaderboard matrices.
  • Email Request: You may also dispatch an email to zipgameapp@gmail.com, and our privacy team will verify and purge all associated server records within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Zip is designed for general audiences and is appropriate for all age brackets. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has provided information without parental consent, contact us at zipgameapp@gmail.com for swift deletion.

6. Your Legal Rights

Under GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679) and applicable international legislation, you maintain rights of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, and data portability.
